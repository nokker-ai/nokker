# Legal guardian attestation

> Draft text, to be reviewed by a lawyer before minor profiles are opened.

By adding a minor profile to my household, I confirm that:

1. I am the legal guardian (parent or tutor) of the minor whose address I enter, or I act with that guardian's
   agreement;
2. I selected the right age band: "teen" from 13 to 17, or "child" under 13;
3. I consent, on behalf of this person, to their use of the assistant under the minor profile rules:
   - their conversations are never used to train or improve a model, and this cannot be changed;
   - no public page, no outbound sending and no irreversible action without my approval, given from MY trusted
     devices;
   - only the connectors and agents I allowed in the household are offered to them;
   - I see their usage (number of messages), not the content of their conversations;
4. I know I can withdraw this consent at any time from "My household": the profile is then suspended, and its data
   can be erased on request.

This attestation is timestamped and tied to the version of this text.

# Brand review

Compare a piece of content with the charter and list concrete deviations. You review; you do not rewrite unless
asked.

## Inputs

- The charter: `brand/charter.md` or the project notes (find them with `memory_search` for "brand charter").
- The content: a text, a list of planned posts, an image file, a page.

## Checks

1. **Name**: written exactly as in the charter, everywhere.
2. **Tone**: matches the three adjectives; no word from the avoid list; the form of address of the language.
3. **Claims**: numbers, client names and promises must be ones the user stated; flag any that are not.
4. **Colours** (visuals and pages): values within the palette; text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text).
5. **Type**: the families and sizes of the charter.
6. **Imagery**: style and subjects of the charter; nothing from the avoid list.
7. **Channel fit**: length and format of the channel (see `social-post-planning` if the social pack is installed).

## Output

| Item | Check | Deviation | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|

Then a one-line verdict: "on brand", "minor fixes" or "rework". If the charter is missing, say so and stop: a
review without a charter is an opinion.

## 2. What the beta is
- A **free** **30-day** test phase, by invitation only, limited to 20 participants.
- Nokker is under development: the service may be interrupted, changed or reset at any time, without notice.
  **No guarantee of availability** or of results.
- We may end your participation if you do not use the service in accordance with these terms.
  You may stop whenever you wish.

## 3. What we ask of you
- Actually use Nokker during the beta and give us your feedback, in particular through a
  **short questionnaire every Sunday evening** (2 minutes; Monday is still fine).
- **Do not entrust sensitive data**: health data, professional or banking secrets, main passwords, data of third
  parties that you have no right to share.
- Do not use Nokker for unlawful purposes or to harm others.
- One account per person; do not share your access or resell it.

## 4. Use of your data to improve our models — optional
This use is **a separate choice**, made at signup with an **unticked** box. You may decline and still take part in the
beta; it is, however, one of the conditions of the benefit in § 9. If you accept:
- your **conversations with Nokker** and **usage statistics** may be used to train and evaluate our models;
- this data is **pseudonymised** before training (name, e-mail address and identifiers removed) and is **never sold**;
- the **content coming from your connectors** (your e-mails, your calendar, your files, the pages visited by the
  browser) is **never** used for training; it is used only to carry out your requests;
- you can **exclude a specific conversation** from this use.

You can **withdraw** this consent at any time in "My account": from then on we stop using your data, and the benefit in
§ 9 ends if it has not yet been earned.

**Important**: once a model has been trained, the information it has learned cannot be removed from it. Erasing your
data (§ 7) deletes your raw data; it does not change models that have already been trained.

## 5. Where your data is processed
- During the beta, the platform is hosted **in France**, with a European provider (OVHcloud): your account, your
  conversations, your connectors and the computations of the AI models. Any payments go through **Stripe**, a payment provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Your beta application remains stored **in Switzerland**.
- An entirely Swiss hosting will be offered later to customers who need it.

## 6. Connectors and actions
- You choose the services you connect (mail, calendar, Telegram…) and you can disconnect them at any time from the
  "Connectors" menu.
- Nokker can act for you with its dedicated browser. **Any action that has an effect** (sending, booking, publishing,
  deleting) **or that commits spending is submitted to you for approval** before it is carried out.
- You remain responsible for the actions you approve.

## 7. Measurements, access and erasure
- We measure the use of the service in an anonymised way (volume, cost, response time, success rate) to improve it.
- For the programme in § 9, we measure **your** weekly activity (active time, number of messages) and keep your
  questionnaire answers; these measurements do not contain the content of your conversations.
- No one on our side reads your conversations, **except with your agreement**, case by case, to resolve an incident.
- **End of the beta**: you can export your data. Unless you move to the paid offer, it is erased within **30 days**,
  subject to § 4.
- You may at any time ask for access to your data, its rectification or its erasure at the address in § 1.

## 8. Included usage
Usage is counted in **messages** (one message = one conversation turn with Nokker). The beta "Solo" offer is
**40 messages per day and 200 per week**, over fixed-time windows shown in "My account" (with the reset time, Zurich time).
During the beta, **we warn you when you go over, without cutting you off**, while these limits are being calibrated;
a safety cap of 150 messages per day still applies against abuse.

## 9. "Solo free for 6 months" benefit
At the end of the 30 days, participants who have **played the game** move to the **free Solo offer for 6 months**.
Conditions, assessed by a member of the team (we measure real use, not time spent with the tab open):
- **active at least 1 hour** in the week, in **3 of the 4 weeks** of the beta;
- **questionnaire completed** on **3 of the 4 Sundays**;
- **use of your data accepted** under § 4 and not withdrawn;
- one account per person, no sharing or resale, within the normal limits of the Solo offer (§ 8).

A **bank card** is requested to activate the benefit, with **no charge at all** during the 6 months. We write to you
**one month before the end** and you can cancel in one click. If you do not cancel, the normal paid subscription then
starts at the price published at that time. Payments are processed by Stripe. The benefit is personal and not
transferable. If you do not meet the conditions, we will propose a suitable offer; it is not automatic.

Some participants may be invited to a **15-minute conversation** about their experience. We **never** quote an opinion
without your written agreement.

## 10. Confidentiality
During the **first two weeks**, please do not publish screenshots or details about the product. After that, you are
free, and even encouraged, to share your experience, without including personal data (yours or anyone else's).

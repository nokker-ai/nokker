# Nokker — Terms of use for minor profiles

> **DRAFT — NOT ACTIVE.** No route serves this text and nobody accepts it. It must be reviewed and approved by a
> lawyer (Swiss and French law at least) before minor profiles are opened (flag `household_minors`). Points to settle:
> see "Open questions" at the end.

**Version 0 (draft) — October 2026**

## 1. Who these terms are for
They complete the Nokker terms for people **under 18** added to a household by their **legal guardian** (parent or
tutor), who holds the subscription ("the payer").

- **"Teen" profile**: 13 to 17.
- **"Child" profile**: under 13.

The legal guardian accepts these terms on behalf of the minor and confirms their age. The guardian remains
responsible.

## 2. Age and consent
- **France**: under **15**, processing a minor's data by an online service requires the joint consent of the minor and
  of the holder of parental authority (French Data Protection Act, art. 45). From 15, the minor may consent alone; the
  profile stays attached to the household.
- **Switzerland**: there is no fixed age; a minor with **capacity of discernment** may exercise their data rights
  themselves (FADP, Civil Code art. 19c). For a "child" profile, the guardian's consent is always required.
- **Elsewhere**: the digital age of consent of the country of residence applies if it is higher.

## 3. What the profile allows, and what it does not
- Talking with the assistant, with a tone and topics suited to the age.
- **No outbound sending** (message, e-mail) and **no irreversible action** without the guardian's approval, given from
  the guardian's own trusted devices.
- **No public page.**
- Only the connectors (calendar, mail…) and agents the guardian allowed in the household are offered.
- No sign-in through a company identity provider (SSO).

## 4. Data
- A minor's conversations are **never** used to train or improve a model. This cannot be changed, neither by the
  minor nor by the guardian.
- They are not copied to the online mirror and stay in the minor's account.
- The guardian sees the profile's **usage** (number of messages), **not the content** of conversations.
- The guardian, or the minor if they have the capacity, can ask for an export or for erasure of the data (contact: see
  the general terms).

## 5. Withdrawing consent
The guardian can withdraw consent at any time from "My household": the profile is **suspended** (no message is
processed) until consent is given again, or erased on request. A minor does not leave the household alone: the
guardian removes them.

## 6. Safety and reporting
The assistant is not a person and does not replace a parent, a teacher or a health professional. In distress, it
points to a trusted adult and to helplines. Inappropriate content can be reported from the app.
